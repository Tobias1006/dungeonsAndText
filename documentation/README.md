## How the game is played
To start of, the player selects a character from four different choices and then sets off on a journey through a small dungeon.
In this dungeon, the player will encounter different rooms containing some story-elements and  some other entities - there will be fighting!
Upon entering a room, the player will be presented with a description of the room they find themselves in and a list of actions they can take. Depending on the character the player chose in the beginning these options, and their outcome, may vary!
The game ends with a final input by the player after defeating the entity lurking in the final room - or if the player parishes in the bowls of the dungeon....
